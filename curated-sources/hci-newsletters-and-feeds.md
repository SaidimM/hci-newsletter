## 2. Leading Academic Labs to Follow (Essential for PhD Exploration)

Following lab blogs and publication pages helps identify potential advisors and research trends:

1. **Carnegie Mellon University (CMU HCII)** - *Human-Computer Interaction Institute*
   - Focus: Broadest spectrum; AI-augmented learning, technical tools, social computing.
2. **Stanford University (Stanford HCI Group)**
   - Focus: Interactive AI (Generative Agents, Michael Bernstein, Maneesh Agrawala, James Landay).
3. **University of Washington (DUB Group - Design Use Build)**
   - Focus: Accessibility (Wobbrock), computing education (Amy Ko), interactive data (Jeffrey Heer).
4. **MIT CSAIL (HCI Engineering Group / Fluid Interfaces)**
   - Focus: Personal fabrication (Stefanie Mueller), augmented perception (Pattie Maes).
5. **UC Berkeley (BID - Berkeley Institute of Design)**
   - Focus: Creative computing, design interfaces (Bjoern Hartmann, Eric Paulos).
6. **Georgia Tech (GVU Center)**
   - Focus: Ubicomp, augmented reality, human-centered computing.

## 3. Top Academic Conferences (The Primary Currency in HCI)

Unlike many traditional fields that prioritize journals, **computer science and HCI prioritize peer-reviewed conference proceedings**:

- **CHI** (May each year): The flagship conference. Covers all sub-fields.
- **UIST** (October each year): Premier systems/tools venue. Best match for software engineers.
- **CSCW** (October/November): Collaborative systems, social dynamics, online communities.
- **IUI** (March): Intelligent user interfaces, explainable AI, recommender interfaces.
- **DIS** (June/July): Focuses on design processes, physical computing, and aesthetics.
