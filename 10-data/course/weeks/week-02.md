## 📝 Homework 02 — Heuristic Evaluation of a Developer Tool

Choose a developer tool you use (Docker Desktop, AWS Console, Postman, GitKraken, Jira, your IDE…).
Define **2 realistic tasks**, walk through them **twice** (first pass: get the flow; second pass: inspect each screen against the 10 heuristics).

**Deliverables**

- [ ] Task definitions (2)
- [ ] Table of **≥ 8 violations**: screen, heuristic, description, severity 0–4, screenshot
- [ ] Top 3 problems with concrete redesign proposals (sketch or annotated screenshot)
- [ ] 1 paragraph: where does the tool's conceptual model clash with your mental model?

**Rubric (/15)**

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| ≥ 8 genuine violations (not personal taste) | /4 |
| Each mapped to the correct heuristic with evidence | /3 |
| Severity ratings justified (frequency × impact × persistence) | /3 |
| Redesigns concrete and feasible | /3 |
| Mental-model reflection shows insight | /2 |

**Grading:** ≥ 13 = A · ≥ 10 = B · below that → redo the weakest criterion before moving on.
