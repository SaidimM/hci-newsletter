## 📝 Homework 11 — Audit an AI Tool Against the 18 Guidelines

Pick an AI product you use (Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT/Claude, Notion AI…). Define 3 tasks, use it, and rate it against **all 18 guidelines** (violated / partially / applied, with evidence).
Then: how could AI responsibly appear in **your semester project**?

**Deliverables**

- [ ] Task list + evidence screenshots
- [ ] 18-row guideline table with ratings and evidence
- [ ] Top 3 failures + redesign proposals
- [ ] 1 page: AI in your semester project — risks, guidelines that apply

**Rubric (/15)**

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| All 18 guidelines assessed with evidence | /4 |
| Ratings are fair and justified | /3 |
| Redesigns address root causes | /3 |
| Project reflection shows critical thinking | /3 |
| Clear writing | /2 |

**Grading:** ≥ 13 = A · ≥ 10 = B · below that → redo the weakest criterion before moving on.
