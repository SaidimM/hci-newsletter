## 📝 Homework 01 — Everyday Gulfs Field Observation

Pick **3 everyday interfaces** (at least 1 physical — e.g. a ticket machine, microwave, elevator, self-checkout — and 1 software).
For each, analyze where the **gulf of execution** and **gulf of evaluation** appear.
For **one** of them, **observe one other person** using it (with their permission) and write down every hesitation, error, and recovery.

**Deliverables**

- [ ] One section per interface: photo/screenshot, user goal, 7-stage walkthrough, where the gulfs are
- [ ] Observation log of 1 real person (P1): timeline of actions, hesitations, errors
- [ ] For each interface: 1 concrete redesign idea that narrows a gulf
- [ ] ~800–1200 words

**Rubric (/15)**

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Gulf analysis is accurate and tied to Norman's vocabulary | /4 |
| Observation of a real person is detailed (actions, timing, quotes) | /4 |
| Redesign ideas directly address the identified gulf | /3 |
| Evidence (photos/screenshots) supports claims | /2 |
| Clear, concise writing | /2 |

**Grading:** ≥ 13 = A · ≥ 10 = B · below that → redo the weakest criterion before moving on.
