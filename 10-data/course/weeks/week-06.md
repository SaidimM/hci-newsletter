## 📝 Homework 06 — 10 Sketches → 2 Paper Prototypes → 4 Tests (Semester Project Part 3)

Generate **10 distinct sketches** answering your HMW questions (quantity first). Pick **2 different directions** and build paper (or Figma low-fi) prototypes.
Test each with **2 people** (4 sessions total) using a simple task + think-aloud.

**Deliverables**

- [ ] Photos of 10 sketches with 1-line captions
- [ ] Rationale for the 2 chosen directions
- [ ] Prototype photos + task given to testers
- [ ] Test notes (P4–P7), what worked/failed per direction
- [ ] Decision: which direction (or hybrid) moves to hi-fi, and why

**Rubric (/15)**

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| 10 genuinely different ideas (not variations of 1) | /3 |
| Chosen directions tied to requirements | /3 |
| Prototypes testable and task-focused | /3 |
| Test findings concrete, with quotes/observations | /4 |
| Clear, evidence-based decision | /2 |

**Grading:** ≥ 13 = A · ≥ 10 = B · below that → redo the weakest criterion before moving on.
