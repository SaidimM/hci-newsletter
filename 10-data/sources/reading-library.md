# HCI Starter Curriculum for Software Engineers

This curated reading list bridges software engineering expertise with HCI academic rigor.

---

## Module 1: Foundational HCI Concepts (Weeks 1–3)

| Paper / Book | Authors | Key Topic | Why Read It |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Design of Everyday Things** (Book) | Don Norman | Affordances, signifiers, mental models, feedback loops | The seminal vocabulary book for modern design & interaction. |
| **Direct Manipulation Interfaces** (1985) | Edwin Hutchins, James Hollan, Donald Norman | Semantic and articulatory distance | Explains why graphical interfaces feel natural compared to CLI. |
| **The Computer for the 21st Century** (1991) | Mark Weiser | Ubiquitous Computing (Ubicomp) | Vision of calm technology and seamless computing. |
| **As We May Think** (1945) | Vannevar Bush | The Memex, associative trails | Historical root of hypertext, personal knowledge, and HCI. |

---

## Module 2: HCI Research Methodology (Weeks 4–6)

| Resource | Author / Venue | Key Topic | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Research Methods in Human-Computer Interaction** (Book) | Jonathan Lazar et al. | Experimental design, qualitative & quantitative analysis | The standard textbook for HCI study methodology. |
| **Qualitative HCI Research** (Guides) | Various / CHI Courses | Semi-structured interviews, thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke) | Learning how to analyze user behavior without statistical bias. |
| **Evaluating User Interfaces: SUS & NASA-TLX** | Brooke / Hart | Usability and cognitive load metrics | Standard measurement instruments in HCI papers. |

---

## Module 3: Systems HCI & Developer Tools (Weeks 7–9)
*(High leverage for software engineers)*

| Paper | Venue | Core Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **The Toolsmith Collaboration** (Brooks) | Classic | How computer scientists can build tools that empower other disciplines. |
| **Vega-Lite: A Grammar of Interactive Graphics** (Satyanarayan et al.) | IEEE TVCG | Creating domain-specific languages (DSLs) for interaction and visualization. |
| **OmniCode / Online Python Tutor** (Guo et al.) | ACM UIST / CHI | Program comprehension through interactive execution traces. |

---

## Module 4: Contemporary Human-AI Interaction & Agents (Weeks 10–12)

| Paper | Venue | Core Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction** (Amershi et al.) | ACM CHI 2019 | 18 empirical design guidelines for AI experiences (Microsoft Research). |
| **Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior** (Park et al.) | ACM UIST 2023 | Memory architectures + LLM-driven virtual worlds. |
| **PromptMaker / Sensemaking with LLMs** | Recent CHI/UIST | How users formulate intent, handle hallucinations, and debug AI outputs. |
