# Social Media Post Templates for Learning in Public

Use these templates to quickly convert paper notes and newsletter digests into engaging public content (X/Twitter threads, LinkedIn articles, Substack newsletters, or visual carousel notes).

---

## Format A: The "SWE Meets HCI" X / Twitter Thread

**Tweet 1 (Hook + Contrarian angle)**:
> Most software engineers think UI design is just CSS and button layout.
> 
> But studying Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) made me realize why our code breaks user mental models.
> 
> Here is a 3-minute breakdown of [Paper / Concept Name] and what every dev should know: 🧵👇

**Tweet 2 (The Core Problem & Context)**:
> [Describe the real friction users face with current systems].
> 
> Researchers at [Lab / University] tackled this in their [Conference] paper...

**Tweet 3 (The System / Solution)**:
> Instead of [conventional way], they introduced [novel interaction paradigm]:
> • Key mechanic 1
> • Key mechanic 2
> 
> Here's what makes this clever from a technical standpoint: [engineering insight].

**Tweet 4 (The Empirical Result)**:
> In a study of [N] users, the results were counter-intuitive:
> • [Surprising finding]
> • [Metric improvement or cognitive difference]

**Tweet 5 (Takeaway for Devs + Call to Discussion)**:
> Big takeaway: Don't just optimize latency; optimize [cognitive load / semantic distance].
> 
> Full paper link: [URL]
> 
> I'm an engineer self-studying HCI on the road to a PhD. Follow along if you're into human-centered software systems! 🚀

## Format B: Weekly Newsletter Digest (Substack / Blog)

**Title**: HCI Dispatch #[N]: [Catchy Topic / "Can AI replace direct manipulation?"]

### 1. The Big Idea of the Week
[300-word explanation of one central HCI principle or controversial debate].

### 2. Paper of the Week (Deep Dive)
- **Paper**: [Title + Venue]
- **The Core Question**: [What question are they answering?]
- **Why It Matters to Practitioners**: [Practical takeaway]
- **My Engineer's Take**: [Critique or prototype idea]

### 3. Cool Prototypes & Newsletters Around the Web
- [Link 1 + 1-sentence commentary]
- [Link 2 + 1-sentence commentary]

### 4. What I'm Building / Reading Next Week
[Brief update on progress].

## Format C: Visual Carousel / Card Format (LinkedIn / Instagram / Xiaohongshu)

- **Slide 1 (Cover)**: Big bold title: *"Why Good Code Still Creates Bad UX (An HCI Perspective)"*
- **Slide 2 (Concept)**: Norman's Gulf of Execution vs Gulf of Evaluation.
- **Slide 3 (Diagram / Visual)**: Flowchart of user goal -> system action -> feedback.
- **Slide 4 (The Solution)**: 3 actionable heuristics.
- **Slide 5 (Summary & Discussion)**: "What's the most annoying UI pattern you encounter daily?"
